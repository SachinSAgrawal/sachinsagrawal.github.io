Privacy Policy

Tabletop Life Counter for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 9 September 2026

Tabletop Life Counter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network connections of any kind.

What the app does not collect

The app has no sign-in, no user profiles, and no server component. It does not gather your name, email address, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, or crash reports. It contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Every framework it uses is supplied by Apple as part of iOS.

What the app accesses, and why

Nothing. The app asks for no permissions at all: no camera, no microphone, no photos, no location, no contacts, and no motion data. It keeps the screen awake while a game is running, which requires no permission and reveals nothing about you.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your current game (player names, colors, life totals, counter values, the change history, the game clock, and your settings) on the device itself, so that a game is still there when you reopen the app.

  • This information stays on your device.
  • It is never sent anywhere. The app makes no network connections at all.
  • The developer has no access to it.

Deleting the app removes this information. If your device backs itself up to iCloud or a computer, the saved game is included in that backup, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

Children

Tabletop Life Counter is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Third parties

There are no third-party services, analytics providers, advertising networks, or data processors involved in this app. No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it accesses, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
